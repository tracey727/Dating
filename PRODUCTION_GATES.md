# Production launch gates

This package is a deployable product foundation, not legal clearance to open a public dating service.

Do not launch publicly until all gates are complete:

- [ ] Australian lawyer reviews Terms, Privacy Policy, Community Standards, moderation process and consumer/payment terms.
- [ ] Privacy Impact Assessment completed for identity documents, matching data, sexuality/gender data, location and safety reports.
- [ ] Confirm Privacy Act / APP obligations and Notifiable Data Breach process.
- [ ] Assess applicable eSafety Online Safety Codes/Standards and align with the Voluntary Online Dating Code / Safety by Design expectations.
- [ ] Specialist identity-verification vendor contracted and privacy terms reviewed.
- [ ] 18+ age-assurance design legally reviewed.
- [ ] Human moderation and escalation coverage exists before users can report harm.
- [ ] Law-enforcement request process and evidence-preservation policy documented.
- [ ] Scam/fraud detection and appeals procedure tested.
- [ ] Cybersecurity threat model and independent penetration test completed.
- [ ] Backups, disaster recovery, monitoring and alerting tested.
- [ ] Payment/refund/cancellation language reviewed under Australian Consumer Law.
- [ ] Insurance reviewed: cyber, public liability, professional/technology liability as appropriate.
- [ ] Accessibility testing completed.
- [ ] LGBTQIA+ and diverse-user safety testing completed.
- [ ] Load test performed at expected concurrent-user levels.
- [ ] App Store / Google Play policies reviewed before mobile release.
